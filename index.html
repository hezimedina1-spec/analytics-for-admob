Analytics for AdMob

Your AdMob earnings, ad performance and revenue trends in one clear dashboard on your Android device.

What the app does

  • Shows your estimated AdMob earnings: today, yesterday, this month and last month.
  • Breaks down revenue by ad unit and format: banner, interstitial, rewarded, native and app-open ads.
  • Displays requests, impressions and daily revenue trends over time.

How it works

You sign in with your own Google account. The app then reads your AdMob reporting data through the official Google AdMob API, in read-only mode, and displays it to you. Your data is shown only to you, on your device. It is never collected, stored on our servers, shared or sold.
