Privacy Policy

Analytics for AdMob

This Privacy Policy describes how the Android application Analytics for AdMob ("the App", "we") handles your information. We built the App with a simple principle: your data belongs to you and stays with you.

1. Information the App accesses

When you sign in with your Google account, the App requests read-only access to your Google AdMob reporting data through the official Google AdMob API. This includes:

  • Estimated earnings and revenue reports of your own AdMob account.
  • Ad performance metrics such as requests, impressions, clicks and eCPM.
  • The list of your AdMob apps and ad units, as needed to display reports.

The App does not access your Gmail, contacts, files, or any other Google data.

2. How your information is used

The data retrieved from the AdMob API is used for one purpose only: displaying your own reports to you, inside the App, on your device.

3. Storage, sharing and transfer of Google user data

  • Your AdMob data is fetched directly from Google's servers to your device over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).
  • We do not operate any server that receives, collects or stores your data.
  • We do not share, transfer, sell or use your data for advertising, marketing, analytics of our own, or any other purpose.
  • Temporary caching of report data may occur locally on your device only, and is removed when you sign out or uninstall the App.
  • Your Google sign-in is handled by Google's official authentication service. The App never sees or stores your password.

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Revoking access

You can revoke the App's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Signing out of the App or uninstalling it removes any locally cached data.

5. Children's privacy

The App is a professional tool intended for AdMob publishers and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

6. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, the updated version will be published on this page.
